Fifteen years of practice across the courtroom and the arbitral table.

R. Venkatesan is an advocate and arbitrator based in Chennai with more than fifteen years of experience in corporate and legal practice. His work spans both courtroom litigation and Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), including commercial and civil matters before the Madras High Court and other judicial forums.

As a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIArb, UK), he offers specialised services in arbitration, mediation and online dispute resolution (ODR), helping parties reach efficient, binding resolutions without the cost and delay of prolonged litigation. Clients are briefed clearly, expectations are set at the outset, and fees are discussed and agreed in writing before work begins.

Discourage litigation wherever you can. Point out how the nominal winner is often a real loser — in fees, in time, and in peace of mind.

The practice covers arbitration and mediation, cheque bounce matters under the Negotiable Instruments Act, civil litigation, criminal defence, family and divorce matters, and the drafting of contracts and legal documents — with particular attention to real estate, finance and commercial disputes. Whether appearing in court or sitting as arbitrator, the aim is the same: protect the client’s interests with strategy and precision.

§ 03 — How I Work

A clear engagement, from first call to resolution.

No surprises on scope or fees. Every instruction follows the same disciplined path.

i.

Free consultation

A first conversation — by call, WhatsApp or in chambers — to understand your matter and whether I am the right counsel for it.

ii.

Written scope & fee

Before any work begins, the scope, likely timeline and fee are set out in writing, so you know exactly what to expect.

iii.

Strategy & drafting

Facts are marshalled, options weighed, and arguments tested carefully before they reach the bench or the tribunal.

iv.

Resolution

Representation through to hearing, award or settlement — in court, in arbitration, or online — always with your interests first.
